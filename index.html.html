A story within the story / How games became making

First, I learned to play.
Then I wanted to build.

Gaming was where my curiosity started. I spent years pushing myself in competitive games, learning their systems from the inside, and asking what made them work. Eventually, the question changed: not only “How do I get better?” but “How did someone make this?”

Chapter 01
Fortnite · Competitive years

Chasing the Unreal rank.

For many years, Fortnite was a big part of my gaming life. I reached Unreal rank and learned what it takes to keep improving: practice, patience, quick decisions, and the willingness to come back for another match.

Chapter 02
Minecraft · A new challenge

A different world. The same drive.

Over time, I shifted toward games like Minecraft. I climbed to high ranks there too, becoming an LT3 player in most game modes. Each mode brought its own strategies and skills, and I loved figuring out how its pieces fit together.

Chapter 03
Development · The next level

From playing worlds to making them.

The more I played, the more interested I became in the design behind the experience: the mechanics, the rules, the small choices that make a game feel right. That curiosity turned into an ambition to work in game development. I started learning coding so I could move from imagining how games might work to building interactive things myself.
